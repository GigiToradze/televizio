# Channel logos

Thirteen real broadcaster logos, supplied by the operator. Four are SVG (1tv, bbc,
euronews, natgeo); the rest are PNG exported at 120px tall with the artboard trimmed
to the mark.

## Prepared for a black background

Most of these were drawn for white backgrounds, so several needed adapting. What was
changed, and why:

| Logo | Change |
|---|---|
| `formula`, `discovery` | Monochrome black marks, inverted to white |
| `cartoon` | Black plate with a white C — inverted, giving the white plate the brand uses on dark |
| `bbc` | The three boxes filled white; the letters stay knocked out |
| `natgeo` | Yellow frame kept, the black wordmark taken to white |
| `euronews` | `#005587` sits at luma 60 and all but vanishes on black — same hue lifted to `#2E93D9` |
| `imedi` | Brand blue lifted 1.55× for the same reason |
| `1tv`, `cnn`, `nickelodeon`, `rustavi2`, `setanta`, `palitranews` | Untouched — they already read on black |

`setanta` and `palitranews` carry their own background plates, which is how they were
supplied. Setanta's black half simply merges into the page; Palitra's white plate reads
as a card.

If a broadcaster supplies an official reverse-on-dark version, use it and drop the
adaptation — these are adjustments made to fit the design, not official variants.

## Replacing one

Keep the filename. Trim the artboard to the mark, export around 120px tall, and check
nothing in it is near-black. The strip caps each logo at 32px tall and 128px wide, so
a wordmark and a square mark end up carrying similar weight.
